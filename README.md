# crystal-bci

What [membrane-crystallization](https://github.com/sparkainlp-x/membrane-crystallization) taught, applied to biosignal commands: a peak-triggered Hebbian learner extracts a blink template ("the crystal") from a noisy **synthetic** multichannel stream — unsupervised — then a Youden-calibrated, fail-closed gate turns blinks into a `next` command, or stays silent.

**SYNTHETIC signals only. Not a medical device. Not evaluated on human recordings.** The device-data adapter that would read real EOG (or similar) does not exist in this repo.

## The one-paragraph version

The crystallization build showed that Hebbian learning recovers structure while the residue is measured. Applied to event detection, that needed a correction: the template lives in the **first** moment (the spike-triggered average), because every blink shares nearly the same shape — the covariance around the mean carries almost nothing about it (measured on the generator: about 2% of variance along the true blink direction). So the crystal is the learned mean, the covariance describes what is left, and a frozen gate decides. Blinks in the generator are **salient** (amp 1.8–2.4); a buried-blink design was falsified and rejected (see DESIGN.md).

## Measured in this repo

**Multi-seed (20 seeds).** Learn on a full stream, calibrate tau on the first half, evaluate on the second half. The ± after each mean is the **standard error (SE)** of the mean (SD / √20), not the seed-to-seed spread. The SD, median and range columns show that spread. Seed 7 alone is the original demo draw, reported for comparison, not as the distribution.

| metric | mean ± SE (20 seeds) | SD | median | min–max | seed 7 alone |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| cosine(crystal, true blink direction) | 0.665 ± 0.033 | 0.149 | 0.682 | 0.278–0.892 | 0.784 |
| residue whiteness | 0.296 ± 0.004 | 0.017 | 0.293 | 0.275–0.322 | 0.279 |
| gate tau | 0.398 ± 0.036 | 0.162 | 0.403 | 0.136–0.626 | 0.522 |
| Youden's J | 0.719 ± 0.021 | 0.092 | 0.714 | 0.496–0.930 | 0.667 |
| eval hit rate | 0.961 ± 0.011 | 0.050 | 1.000 | 0.882–1.000 | 1.000 |
| eval false alarms | 18.6 ± 10.9 | 48.6 | 1.5 | 0–218 | 0 |
| blink-free control false alarms (12000 ticks) | 24.0 ± 13.3 | 59.5 | 0.5 | 0–267 | 0 |
| fixed-template hit | 0.878 ± 0.029 | 0.128 | 0.913 | 0.500–1.000 | 0.765 |
| fixed-template false alarms (A/B) | 86.5 ± 24.5 | 109.6 | 47.0 | 0–400 | 114 |

**False alarms are heavy-tailed.** The mean of 18.6 is driven by a few seeds: the median is 1.5, 10 of 20 seeds had none, and one seed had 218. For this metric, read the median and range rather than the mean ± SE. Paired per seed, the fixed template had 67.9 more false alarms than the crystal on average (SD 128.1, SE 28.6), and the crystal had more false alarms in 2 of 20 seeds; its hit rate was lower in 4 of 20 seeds (mean difference +0.083, SD 0.155). With only 20 seeds and skewed counts, treat the A/B comparison as indicative.

**Honest reading.** The learned crystal still beats a fixed canonical template on average (higher hit rate, fewer false alarms). Seed 7's perfect hit/FA rates and zero blink-free alarms are **above the mean**: across seeds the frozen gate is imperfect, and fail-closed silence on blink-free streams is not guaranteed for every seed. Full table (regenerated by `python sweep.py > results_sweep.md`): [results_sweep.md](results_sweep.md).

## Honest limits

- **Synthetic signals only.** Biphasic pulses + white noise + drift are not physiology. No human, no headset, no electrodes.
- **Not a medical device.** Not a diagnostic or treatment aid.
- **The device-data adapter is still missing.** This is the learn-and-gate half of a future pipeline; the half that reads real recordings does not exist here.
- One command class (`next`). Multi-command is future work.
- Residue whiteness ~0.30: leftover structure remains (noise-triggered windows). An adaptive trigger is the next experiment, not claimed here.
- Classical simulation, stdlib only, seeded.

See [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) for decisions and four designs falsified along the way.
